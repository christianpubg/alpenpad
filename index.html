How it worksSIMD-0326 · Votor

Solana just got final in 150ms.Alpenpad is built around it.

Alpenglow is the biggest consensus change in Solana's history. It retires TowerBFT and Proof of History as the consensus clock, and replaces them with Votor. Finality goes from about 12.8 seconds to about 150 milliseconds. Alpenpad is the first launchpad designed around that number.

01Votor

Votes become certificates.
One round, or two.

Validators stop writing votes into blocks. They send them straight to each other and aggregate them into certificates. A block finalizes on one of two paths that run at the same time.

Fast path1 round
80% of stake votes

If at least 80% of stake notarizes a block in the first round, it's final right there.

Round 1Final
Slow path2 rounds
60%, then 60% again

If round one lands between 60% and 80%, a second round runs. 60% again and the block is final.

02Launching on Alpenpad

From idea to finalized coin.
Four steps, one signature.

01

Create

Name, ticker, image and a line about it. Links if you have them. Set a dev buy or leave it at zero.

02

Sign once

Mint, curve and your dev buy go out in a single transaction from your own wallet.

03

Finalized

Alpenpad waits for the finalized certificate, not just a confirmation. On Alpenglow that's ~150ms. You see the exact time.

04

Curve, then graduate

Trading runs on a bonding curve. When the curve fills, the coin graduates to an AMM pool.

03Why finality matters for a launch

Confirmed is a promise.
Finalized is a fact.

Your launch can't be undone

Once the certificate lands, your mint and dev buy are permanent. No waiting 12 seconds to know for sure.

Charts on final state

The board and every chart read finalized commitment. What you see is what settled, not what might.

Graduation in real time

When a curve fills, the migration finalizes just as fast. Holders see the pool the moment it exists.
